# Division

## div1.asm
Operation: 100 divided by 7 gives quotient 14 and remainder 2.

 The quotient is stored in AL and the remainder in AH.

 CF, OF, SF, ZF, AF, and PF are undefined after the DIV instruction, so their displayed values should not be treated as meaningful.

## div2.asm
Operation: 50000 divided by 300 gives quotient 166 and remainder 200.

 The quotient is stored in AX and the remainder in DX.

 CF, OF, SF, ZF, AF, and PF are undefined after the DIV instruction, so their displayed values should not be treated as meaningful.
